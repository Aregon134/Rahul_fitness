A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Your questions.
Answered.

What is the X Frame Method?

A step-by-step fitness system and private Skool community focused on building muscle, losing fat and developing your physique naturally.

Do I need supplements?

Rahul’s approach is built around training, food and consistency. Supplements are not a requirement of the method.

Who is it for?

The community welcomes adults at different starting points: new lifters, people returning to training, and those looking for more structure.

What does the $29/year membership include?

Structured workout programs, simple nutrition modules, training and mindset resources, guidance from Rahul, a private fitness community and future updates.

Are 1–1 coaching and the diet plan included?

They are separate offers. Ask Rahul for their pricing, availability and confirmed inclusions before purchasing.

Where do I access the membership?

The X Frame Method is hosted on Skool. The membership button takes you to Rahul’s Skool page to review the offer, join and access the community.

Can I cancel?

The membership is advertised as cancel anytime. Review the current renewal, cancellation and payment details on Skool before joining.
